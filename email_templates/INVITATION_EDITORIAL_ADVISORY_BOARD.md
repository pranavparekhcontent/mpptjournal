### **About the Journal:**
*Journal of Modern Pharmacy Praxis and Therapeutics* (MPPT Journal) bridges rigorous scientific laboratory research with practical real-world pharmacy applications and clinical outcomes (*praxis*). Our core publishing pillars are:
* **Integrity & Independence:** Strictly compliant with the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and ICMJE guidelines.
* **Rapid & Rigorous Peer Review:** Double-blind evaluation with transparent turnaround times (14–21 days).
* **Global Open Access:** Ensuring published scholarship is universally accessible to clinicians, researchers, and students without barriers.

### **Role & Expectations of Advisory Board Members:**
Recognizing your demanding academic schedule, the board commitment is designed to be prestigious, structured, and manageable:
1. **Strategic Advice:** Provide occasional input on journal scope, emerging topics, or special thematic issues (via email/virtual annual meeting).
2. **Occasional Peer Review:** Evaluate 2 to 3 high-priority manuscripts per year in your specialized domain.
3. **Ambassadorship:** Encourage colleagues, postdocs, and research scholars in your network to consider MPPT Journal for publishing high-caliber research.
4. **Statutory Listing:** Your name, institutional affiliation, and ORCID will be prominently accredited on the official journal masthead, website, and ISSN regulatory filings.
5. **Honorary Appointment:** This is an honorary academic position; formal Certificate of Editorial Appointment will be issued.
