### **Why Review for MPPT Journal?**
Peer reviewers are the bedrock of scientific credibility. In appreciation of your valuable contribution, MPPT Journal offers:
1. **Formal Reviewer Accreditation:** Receive an official, verifiable digital **Certificate of Peer Review Excellence** for every manuscript successfully evaluated.
2. **Editorial Board Priority:** Active and dedicated reviewers are given prime consideration for induction into the journal’s Section Editorial Board.
3. **APC Waivers / Discounts:** Special publication fee waivers for future manuscripts submitted by our active reviewers.
4. **Web of Science / Publons Recognition:** Official verification and credit for your academic peer-review record.

---

### **Reviewer Commitments & Ethical Standards:**
* **Double-Blind Standard:** All reviews are strictly double-blind (author and reviewer identities remain mutually confidential).
* **Manageable Workload:** You will receive no more than **3 to 5 manuscripts per year**, strictly aligned with your stated keywords.
* **Flexible Deadlines:** Standard review period is **14 to 21 days**. You maintain full discretion to accept or decline any individual review assignment based on your availability.
* **Ethics Compliance:** Adherence to the ethical guidelines established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).
