### **Key Responsibilities & Operational Scope:**

As Associate Editor, you will serve as the cornerstone of our editorial desk:

1. **Initial Desk Screening:** Screen incoming submissions for scope conformity, author guidelines adherence, and plagiarism reports (similarity threshold &lt; 15%).
2. **Peer Review Coordination:** Identify and assign two (2) independent, double-blind peer reviewers per manuscript matching their research specialties.
3. **Turnaround Monitoring:** Monitor review progress, ensure timely submission of referee reports (target 14-day turnaround), and send gentle review reminders.
4. **Editorial Recommendations:** Synthesize reviewer comments into clear editorial recommendations (*Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Reject*) for final approval by the Editor-in-Chief.
5. **Author Liaison:** Communicate revision requests and technical requirements professionally with corresponding authors.
