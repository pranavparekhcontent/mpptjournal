From the Editor-in-Chief

A letter to our first authors.

Dear Colleague,

Every journal begins as a promise. Ours is simple: rigorous, reproducible pharmaceutical science — reviewed by active doctorate-level researchers, decided in fourteen to twenty-one days, and read by everyone, everywhere, for free.

We built MPPT because too much good science waits too long behind tolls and opaque queues. The inaugural volume carries no article processing charges, no hidden costs, and no compromise: double-blind review aligned with COPE benchmarks, a similarity ceiling of fifteen percent, and continuous publication upon acceptance.

If your work moves evidence from molecule to bedside — in pharmacology, delivery science, clinical praxis, computational design, phytotherapy, or regulatory analysis — this pages are yours.

— Dr. Pranav ParekhEditor-in-Chief, MPPT Journal

Six disciplines, one standard.

Pharmacology & Toxicology

Cellular and molecular mechanisms of drug action, receptor pharmacodynamics, neuro- and immunopharmacology, toxicological safety screening.

№ 02

Pharmaceutics & Novel Drug Delivery

Peer Review & Author Guidelines

The protocol, in four movements.

i.
Days 1–3

Desk Screening

Scope verification and similarity analysis; maximum allowable similarity <15%, excluding bibliography.

ii.
Days 4–14

Double-Blind Review

Anonymized manuscripts assigned to two independent doctorate-holding reviewers with matched expertise.

iii.
Days 15–21

Adjudication

The Editor-in-Chief issues the official decision — accept, minor or major revision, or reject — with actionable feedback.

iv.
Continuous

Publication

Professional XML/PDF typesetting and immediate online publication with persistent identifiers.
